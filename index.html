Cube notation

Hold the cube with the U face on top and F facing you. Letters are clockwise quarter turns from that hold.

Faces

R
Right
L
Left
U
Up
D
Down
F
Front
B
Back

Modifiers

R
90° clockwise
R'
90° counter-clockwise (prime)
R2
180°
R2'
180° the other way (same end)

Wide moves

r
Right + middle (Rw)
l
Left + middle (Lw)
u
Up + middle (Uw)
d
Down + middle (Dw)
f
Front + middle (Fw)
b
Back + middle (Bw)

Slices

M
Middle, like L (between L & R)
E
Equator, like D (between U & D)
S
Standing, like F (between F & B)

Cube rotations

x
Whole cube like R
y
Whole cube like U
z
Whole cube like F

Tips

  • Lowercase = wide (two layers).
  • Parentheses group fingertricks, e.g. (R U R').
  • Leading y / y' / y2 often means rotate before the alg.
